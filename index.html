Set your grid size, then click or drag across the squares to fill them in. Drag over filled squares to clear them again. Try the mesh brush for a special stitch or a pop of color. Use the + and − around the grid to add or remove rows and columns, Rotate to start from any corner, Select to fill, erase, move, copy and paste blocks, Add → Enter Text to turn a word or name into a draft pattern, Add → Border to frame your design, and the tabs above the grid to keep several designs going at once. When you're happy, download the image to print it or check off rows as you hook!
